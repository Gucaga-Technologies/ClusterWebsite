MB Nanofibre

Welcome to MB Nanofibre

MB Nanofibre is a PharmaIT Cluster partner focused on MTEP/R&D project administration, EU consulting, compliance support, and export development in Central and Eastern Europe. The company helps structure R&D initiatives and supports teams with grant documentation, reporting, and market-entry coordination.

What MB Nanofibre Does

  • Administers and consults external R&D teams.
  • Supports EU R&D project structuring, compliance, and grant reporting.
  • Provides CE, MDR 2017/745, and GDPR-oriented support for relevant projects.
  • Coordinates PharmaIT Cluster export work in Romania, Poland, Bulgaria, and other CEE markets.

Key Facts

  • MTEP startup and PharmaIT Cluster partner.
  • Experience with 3 MITA-approved EU projects.
  • Focus area: R&D administration, EU consulting, and CEE export coordination.
